## 👋 Who We Are?

**ACSES** is the Computer Science & Engineering students community at **Walchand College of Engineering, Sangli**.

A place to **learn technology, build things, compete, collaborate, and grow together.**

> `Not just a club. A community of builders.`

## 🎯 Events & Initiatives

From getting started with technology to competing at a larger scale, ACSES conducts initiatives throughout the year.

### GIM — General Interest Meet

An introduction to the ACSES community for first-year CSE students.

### TECHUMEN

ACSES's flagship mega-event bringing together **technology, competition and creativity.**

### Technical Sessions & Workshops

Workshops, programming sessions, project-based learning and more.

### Beyond Code

Mock interviews, communication, teamwork and personality development.
